Mechanics

How the bones get fetched back

  1. 01

    5% of every trade, into the kennel

    Every buy and sell of $DOGIX pays a 5% fee that goes straight into the index kennel, denominated in USDC. It is written into the contract — there is no manual switch.

    • 5% on buys / 5% on sells
    • Priced in USDC, insulated from token price moves
    • Kennel balance verifiable on-chain
  2. 02

    Buy the top five bones by market cap

    The kennel buys the five largest bones, weighted by market cap, and swaps the lineup once a week — the softest bone is rotated out and a harder one takes its place.

    • Ranked by Arc pool market cap
    • Rebalanced weekly
    • Last place rotates out
  3. 03

    Fetched back into your bowl

    The bones are airdropped to every qualifying wallet, pro rata to holdings. There is no staking page and no claim button — it just shows up.

    • Threshold: 0.1% of supply (100,000 $DOGIX)
    • Cadence: one round every 24 hours
    • Every transfer comes with a txid

    FAQ

    About this dog

    Do I really have to do nothing?

    Right. There is no staking contract and no claim button. As long as your balance stays at or above 0.1% of supply, bones land in your wallet on every round.

    Why the top five and not the top ten?

    Harder bones are better to chew. The top five concentrate almost all of Arc's liquidity, so the weighting is tighter and the rotation is cleaner — drop out of the ranking and you are out.

    What does it cost to sell?

    5% on buys and 5% on sells. None of it goes to the team — all of it goes into the index kennel to buy the next round of bones.

    Can I sell the airdropped bones right away?

    What lands is a real on-chain token. You can do whatever you want with it, and there is no lock-up.
