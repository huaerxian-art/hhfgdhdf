How it works

Three steps, on repeat

  1. 01

    5% fee on every trade

    Every buy and sell of $DOGIX routes 5% into the index kennel, denominated in USDC. Nobody can pull it back out.

  2. 02

    Buy the top five bones

    The kennel buys the bones in the index — the five hardest bones on Arc, ranked by market cap.

  3. 03

    Fetch them back to holders

    Bones are airdropped straight into your bowl. No staking, no claiming. Hold at least 0.1% of the supply (100,000 $DOGIX) to qualify.
